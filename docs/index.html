About me
Scroll

I am an ecologist and Postdoctoral Researcher at the Doñana Biological Station (EBD-CSIC). My work explores the hidden layers of biodiversity—specifically species interactions within ant, microbiome and mycobiome communities.

Email Google Scholar Instagram Github ORCID Researchgate

Research

Currently, I lead the PRELUDIUM project, where I investigate the role of ants as bacteria dispersal vectors and their indirect impact on plant health. While my PhD at the Museum and Institute of Zoology (Warsaw) was built on studying the multimodal adaptations (chemical, acoustic, and genetic) of social parasites, my current research focuses on species co-occurrence and host-microbiome interactions leveraging long-read sequencing technologies.

Open Science

I am a staunch defender of Open Science, reproducibility and “home-made” lab protocols. I believe in making high-level molecular ecology accessible by bridging the gap between benchwork and computational biology. A core part of my current technical workflow involves Oxford Nanopore sequencing and bioinformatics to streamline data processing from raw amplicon DNA reads to ecological insights.
