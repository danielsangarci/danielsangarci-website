Curriculum vitae

Postdoctoral Researcher at the Doñana Biological Station (EBD-CSIC), Seville, Spain. PhD in Biology (Museum and Institute of Zoology, Warsaw). I study species interactions in ant, microbiome and mycobiome communities.
